One line per class. `Detect` says whether a script catches it (M), a reader must judge it (J),
or a script catches most of it (M~). `Seen` counts confirmed occurrences across reads; two
confirmed occurrences of a J or M~ class means: write the check. Quotes are real findings,
scrubbed of names.

## False positives (do not report these)

- Bold "say this out loud" lines in first person: talking out loud is the skill being taught.
- A frozen figure caption or code comment using a term: define the term BEFORE the figure in prose.
- A deliberately broken code block labelled broken: assert the bug reproduces; do not fix it.
- A drill's bug section (beat 6) without a picture: drills declare up front that they carry no wrong-turn figure.
- The honest inverse of a tenure line, marked as such ("shipped for years; never did this under a clock").

## Scoring (report both, separately, every read)

- **Taught me** 1 to 10: could the reader re-derive the mechanism closed-book from the pictures and traces.
- **Finish without disengaging** 1 to 10: bounded asks, no stalls, no rereads.
A fix pass is done when the second score has not dropped (definitions and caveats add mass).
